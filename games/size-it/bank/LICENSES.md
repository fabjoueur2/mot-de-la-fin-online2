# Licensing

## Natural Earth
Country silhouettes are derived from Natural Earth data. Natural Earth states that all raster and vector map data on its website are in the public domain and may be used for personal, educational, and commercial purposes.

## Original geometric SVGs
Paper, sports, standards, and space SVGs in this package were generated specifically for this package from simple geometry. The numeric dimensions are factual data.

## Important
This is an asset/data starter bank, not legal advice. Keep source/license metadata with each entry. Some measurement values are game conventions or rounded reference values and should be independently verified before a public production release.

No Pop Culture characters, logos, or franchise artwork are included.
